Membership

The Association for Evaluation and Accreditation of Language Education Programs currently operates with 43 experienced members who are employed by approximately 30 institutions of higher education. New member admissions are not currently being accepted, and future admissions will be made from individuals who have completed the DEDAK reviewer training and have served at least once on a DEDAK accreditation reviewer team.
